Meticulously curated from single-source local farms around Manachanallur, our Gold Kichadi is natural steam processed to protect vitamins and fibers, double-polished under FSSAI protocols, and electronic-color-sorted to ensure immaculate, premium white grains.
1. Rinse gently. Do not rub the grains to prevent breakage.
2. Soak in clean water for 25-30 minutes for a softer texture.
3. Recommended rice-to-water ratio is 1 : 2.0 (pressure cooker) or 1 : 2.5 (open pot).
4. Let the cooked rice rest in the closed cooker for 10 minutes before fluffing it.

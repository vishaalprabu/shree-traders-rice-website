About Our Products

We supply four premium Manachanallur rice varieties: Manachanallur Ponni Rice (₹1,500/25kg), Manachanallur ADT 36 Rice (₹1,230/25kg), Shree Gold Kichadi Ponni Rice (₹1,950/25kg), and Nesam Kichadi Ponni Rice (₹2,080/25kg). All varieties are FSSAI certified and sourced directly from Cauvery basin farmers in Manachanallur.
Yes. All rice varieties supplied by Shree Traders are 100% FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) certified. We follow strict food safety standards at every stage — from sourcing and storage to packaging and delivery. We can provide our FSSAI certificate upon request.
Ponni rice is a medium-grain variety ideal for everyday South Indian meals like rice with sambar, rasam, and curries. It offers light, fluffy grains with consistent expansion. Kichadi rice (also called Kichadi Ponni) is a slender, super-fine grain variety with a richer fragrance and softer texture, making it the preferred choice for upscale restaurants, weddings, and gourmet dining events.
Our rice is naturally sun-dried and sourced from traditional Cauvery basin farms using minimal processing. While we currently do not carry a formal organic certification, our direct farmer relationships ensure clean, naturally grown paddy with no artificial additives.

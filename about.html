Our Story

From the Rich Delta Fields of Tamil Nadu to Your Family's Table

Shree Traders is a family-run heritage rice business based in Manachanallur, a legendary region celebrated for premium paddy sourcing and trusted agricultural expertise.

Generations of Trust

Built on Quality, Lifelong Farmer Alliances, & Honest Trade

For more than 20 years, our family has operated close to the source, partnering with local Cauvery delta farmers and premium mills to provide pristine rice.

We hand-select pristine paddy varieties and rely on time-tested sun-drying methods to seal in raw aroma, organic flavor, and rich nutritional values. Every grain is cleaned, aged, and milled under strict observation to ensure your home or catering kitchen receives absolute cooking perfection.

Our mission remains unchanged: deliver the wholesome, nutrient-dense soul of traditional South Indian rice fields directly to tables that demand consistent quality.

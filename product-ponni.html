Grown in the rich, fertile soils along the Cauvery river basin, our signature Manachanallur Ponni Rice is aged meticulously to ensure separate, beautifully fluffy grains upon cooking. Famous for its traditional aroma, soft cooking texture, and high expansion ratio, it is the perfect staple for homes and premium food establishments alike.

Rice TypeBoiled Ponni Rice
Grain SizeMedium-slender
Aging Period12+ Months (Aged)
RegionManachanallur, Tamil Nadu
Our Ponni rice is processed using clean, hybrid sun-drying methods at certified local partner mills in Manachanallur. It passes strict FSSAI quality standards, ensuring zero moisture, pristine cleanliness, and uniform grain sizes before packaging.
1. Wash the rice gently 2-3 times in fresh water.
2. Soak the rice for 20-30 minutes prior to cooking for optimal length.
3. Recommended rice-to-water ratio is 1 : 2.5.
4. Cook for 3 whistles in a pressure cooker or steam boil in an open pot until fluffy.
